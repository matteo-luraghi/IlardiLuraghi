- The recommender system is built by S&C, therefore it is not an external user/actor
- The interviews occur on an external platform integrated into the system
- Companies can directly offer an internship to a student only through the recommendations that the system offers them
- The CV is analyzed from the recommendation engine
- the "Company proposals" section contains the internships proposed by companies to the student; the "Suggestions" section contains the internships suggested by the recommendations system.
- The Application strictly refers to the application to obtain an interview. If the application is accepted, the student will receive possible dates for the interview, if the application is rejected there won't be an interview. Because of this, this Application process only applies to students who either proactively found the internship announcement or who got the internship recommended: those who were chosen from the company itself are automatically admitted to an interview since the company already reviewed the necessary information
- At the moment, there is no possibility to reschedule an interview. If for any reason a student cannot comply to the date they chose freely, the outcome of the failed interview will be decided by the company. Once the date is decided both parties are expected to respect the appointment
- Feedback on both students and companies is public for everyone to see, as this is additional information upon which students can decide whether or not to apply for an internship and the same goes for companies
- Feedbacks are mandatory for both parties (the system does not enforce in any way this, but it insists that feedback is expected). Because of this, the first state of the Feedback object is Expected. The interested party who receives the feedback is allowed to optionally answer it if they feel the need to address something in the other party's statement.
- The recommendation system matches internships and students in a user symmetric fashion
